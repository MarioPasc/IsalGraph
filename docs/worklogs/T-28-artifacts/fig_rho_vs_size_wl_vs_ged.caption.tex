Distance fidelity under two references, within a fixed node count. Spearman $\rho$ between each representation's own distance and a reference distance, computed inside strata $n_i=n_j$ so the size channel is removed by construction rather than by adjustment; higher is better. \textbf{(a)} the Weisfeiler--Lehman subtree kernel distance, $\sqrt{K(G,G)+K(H,H)-2K(G,H)}$ on unnormalized colour-count multisets at $h=2$, fitted once per dataset. \textbf{(b)} graph edit distance, reproducing Fig.~\ref{fig:rho-vs-size}: exact GED on the wide axes, where it is computable to $n=12$, and above that the proven lower/upper bracket as small multiples, one per representation with a final panel overlaying every arm. \textbf{The representation distances are identical throughout} --- the same cached matrices enter (a) and (b), and only the reference changes --- so any difference between the halves is a property of the yardstick and not of the encoding. The WL kernel distance is exact at every size, which is why (a) needs no bracket, no ceiling and no regime split; the bracket is kept separate from exact GED in (b) because a proven interval sharing an axis with an exact value invites the reader to read one as the other. In the small multiples, dashed is the lower bound and solid the upper, and the grey envelope behind each is the span of the whole field at that node count. The two halves share one $\rho$ axis and one legend, and both are plotted against graph size $n$; they are \emph{not} on a common $x$ scale, since exact GED spans ten strata where the other references span sixty-two. Points are Fisher-$z$ weighted means across datasets, weighted by graphs ($n-3$) and never by the quadratically many pairs they induce; bands and bars are the corresponding 95\,\% intervals. Open circles mark points surviving Benjamini--Hochberg at $q=0.05$ over all 908 points drawn here, against $H_0\!:\rho=0$; the correction is local to this figure and is not the pre-registered family. \emph{WL subtree is the reference in (a)}, so its distance is the reference by construction and its curve is the identity $\rho\equiv1$ rather than a competitor result; it is drawn rather than hidden, and excluded from every win count taken under this reference. Its curves in (b) are computed from the cached kernel matrices and therefore differ from Fig.~\ref{fig:rho-vs-size}, which carries a defect in the WL count vectors; no other series differs, and no IsalGraph series is affected. Descriptive.
